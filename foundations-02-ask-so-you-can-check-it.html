Ask So You Can Check It

A good prompt isn't a magic phrase. It's one that hands you back something you can go and verify — and then actually use.

Same student, four endings

Every one of these starts:
“I'm a first-generation junior in Missouri with a 3.4 GPA, going into nursing.”

Tap each ending to see what it gets you.

You can't ask AI to vouch for itself. Ask for details you can check yourself.

Then actually check

Suppose it hands you: “The Horizon Scholars Award — $40,000, all 50 states, deadline March 15.” Before anything else, check the thing everything else depends on: does this award exist, and are those details current? Not whether the money is enough. Not your odds. Search the name, find the real page. A scholarship that doesn't exist can't be applied to.
