Make It Even Stronger

Specifics have to replace words, not join them

A one-line cap and "add detail" pull against each other. Every specific you add has to push something vaguer out.

1
Cut the throat-clearing first. "Utilised", "in order to", "various" and "successfully" are pure length. Deleting them buys you a detail.
2
Trade an adjective for a number. "Multiple tools" (14 chars) becomes "4 tools" (7) — shorter and more credible.
3
Keep the verb, drop the scaffolding. The strongest bullets are verb, object, result. Anything else is negotiable.
Too long — wraps to two lines (147)

Successfully utilised various AI tools in order to differentiate a range of science materials for students of differing reading abilities.

⚡ One line, more specific (89)

Levelled a 7th-grade science unit 3 ways with AI so all 28 students met the same core content.
