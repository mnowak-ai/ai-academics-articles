AI Doesn't Look Anything Up

Most people picture AI as a search engine with a really good memory. That picture is wrong — and once you swap it out, almost every strange thing AI does starts making sense.

Three things that follow

  1. It has a cut-off date. Training stopped at some point. Anything after that, it never saw — and it has no way to go and check.
  2. It doesn't stop when it doesn't know. It produces the most likely-sounding answer instead: real-looking names, a plausible score, total confidence. That's a hallucination.
  3. The same skill does useful things. Your photo app builds a “Trip to Chicago” album because it recognised faces, places and dates — not because it read your texts.
Confidence is not a signal. AI sounds exactly as sure when it learned something as when it filled a gap with something that sounds right.
